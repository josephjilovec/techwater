# Water Use Review illustration

`water-use-review.svg` is the primary website image: an accessible vector sample report with fictional records. `water-use-review.png` is a raster export for social sharing.

Period A: 24,000 US gallons / 30 days = 800 US gallons/day. Period B: 31,500 / 35 days = 900 US gallons/day. Billed consumption increased 31.25% (rounded to 31.3%); daily consumption increased 12.5%. The sample does not establish a cause or claim completed client results.

The older `hero.webp` is an unused AI-generated concept of meter hardware, retained as a source asset. It is no longer displayed by the site.
