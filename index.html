01 / The record

A total tells
part of the story.

One fictional bill records 24,000 US gallons over 30 days. The next records 31,500 over 35 days. The units match. The periods do not.

A higher billed total can reflect more than a change in daily use.

02 / The comparison

More water.
Also more days.

Account for the length of each period: 800 US gallons per day becomes 900. The daily increase is 12.5%, compared with a 31.3% rise in the billed total.

A fairer comparison. Still no conclusion about the cause.

03

Periodic reporting has limits. It does not provide real-time leak alerts, establish water safety, or prove conservation savings. No live meter feed or actual customer results are claimed.
