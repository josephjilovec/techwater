REPORTING SERVICE / NO NEW HARDWARE REQUIRED

A readable history.

Consumption, dates, units, and the limits of the available record.

A fairer comparison.

03

A question with an owner.

Keep the next check clear and its result open until someone responds.

Periodic reporting has limits. It does not provide real-time leak alerts, establish water safety, or prove conservation savings. No live meter feed or actual customer results are claimed.
