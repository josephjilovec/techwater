TechWater / Privacy & local interactions

Your records.
Your choice.

What the TechWater concept website does with the information you enter.

The note is assembled locally.

The readiness form creates an editable draft in your browser. It does not upload bills, connect utility accounts, request credentials, or automatically submit the fields to TechWater. Leaving or reloading the page can discard an unsaved note.

You choose the next step.

Copy writes the draft to your clipboard. Download creates a local text file. Open email draft passes the current text to your configured email app, where you review and decide whether to send. Keep the first note general and omit sensitive account or personal information.

Only display preferences are remembered.

The motion control can remember your display choice in this tab’s session storage. The form fields and draft are not saved there. Device reduced-motion preferences are respected.

Examples are not live records.

Sample quantities, periods and follow-ups are fictional. Generated water and property imagery is illustrative, not evidence of a customer property, water-quality result or measured savings. No live account, meter, alerting service or provider network is connected.

Hosting and contact.

Vercel delivers the site’s pages, images, font and scripts. The website adds no analytics, advertising scripts, accounts, payments or automatic form-submission service. If you choose to email, your email provider handles that message separately.

For a general website question, use the existing contact email.
